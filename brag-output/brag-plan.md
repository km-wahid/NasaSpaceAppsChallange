# Brag Plan: ORBIT Crop Rotation Lab

## Planning rubric
1. ORBIT connects district context, farm inputs and reviewed crop references to transparent crop rotation decision support.
2. Strongest claim: “No guesswork hidden behind a recommendation.”
3. Visual hook: the bundled farmland photograph with the hero's large, italic wider-view headline.
4. Show the location and water controls, followed by the growing calendar and comparison UI.
5. 20 seconds is the shortest satisfying film with readable interaction and a calm finish.
6. Polished: warm agricultural product film, confident typography, fast entrances and long holds.
7. Warm bundled music bed with restrained clicks and soft card arrivals.
8. Built ORBIT: NASA-informed crop rotation planning, grounded in your district, water and farming priorities. Compare growing calendars and see the reasons behind each option.
9. Choose a district → select water availability → compare crop rotations and their trade-offs.

## The angle
A wider view becomes a local plan. Open on the actual landscape and product headline, move into the actual planning controls, and end on the calendar. Use source presentation fixtures only with a prominent Sample plan label; these are previews, not a claimed personalized recommendation. No account or private data is used. District selection is illustrative, not device detection.

## Storyboard
1. Wider view, 0–4s: original farm photograph, ORBIT brand and exact hero headline. Gentle camera drift, quick text entrance and at least 2.5s settled hold. Warm music; no interaction. Soft transition.
2. Your field, 4–9s: source-derived location/water controls. “Start with your location”, district Dhaka, “Your water availability”, High / Medium / Low. Cursor selects Medium. Sequential controls arrive with sufficient combined hold. One restrained click. Label this as an illustrative interface.
3. Growing options, 9–16s: source-derived growing calendar, prominently labeled “Sample plan · Seasonal preview”. Three sequential rows: Lentil Nov 2026–Mar 2027; Mungbean Apr–Jun 2027; Aman rice Jul–Nov 2027. Source fixture dates; no invented yields, scores or farm suitability. Show “Compare rotation plans” and “Seasonal water planning / Crop diversity”. Soft arrivals; all rows hold for at least 3s.
4. Clear reasons, 16–20s: ORBIT with exact source copy “A clear reason. Behind every option.” and “Plan my season”. Product-specific final frame with a small calendar preview. Music fades out; soft final accent.
