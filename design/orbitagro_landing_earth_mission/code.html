Frequently Asked Questions & Methodology

Transparent validation standards explaining our satellite algorithms.

How are 1km satellite grids downscaled to individual 10m fields? expand_more

We combine coarse-resolution radiometer data (such as SMAP's 9km soil moisture) with high-resolution Sentinel-2 and Landsat-9 (10m - 30m) multispectral bands using a super-resolution random forest regression algorithm trained on local topography and drainage indexes.

How does the system account for persistent monsoon cloud cover? expand_more

During peak Kharif-2 monsoon storms when optical satellites cannot penetrate clouds, OrbitAgro automatically switches to active microwave Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) from Sentinel-1, which penetrates dense clouds and rain cells to measure ground inundation and crop geometry seamlessly.

Does OrbitAgro work for marginal farmers with no smartphones? expand_more

Yes. Regional dashboards are designed for Village Agriculture Extension Agents (SAAO) and Union Parishad Digital Centers. Summarized advisory bulletins are dispatched via low-bandwidth SMS and voice blasts in Bengali to registered cooperatives.
